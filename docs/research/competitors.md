# Competitor research: My Planner

**Checked: 17 October 2026.** Prices are USD, before tax; annual equivalents require upfront payment. Sources are current official pages and 2025–2026 documentation unless noted. Store prices vary. ADHD benefits and friction below are product-design assessments, not clinical findings. “Unverified” means documentation did not establish the capability.

My Planner’s opportunity: combine useful daily structure, life-task templates and printing with free, local, account-free access.

## Todoist

A flexible task manager for personal and professional work.

- Natural-language capture; lists/boards; priorities; filters.
- Subtasks; recurrence; reminders; paid calendar/time-blocking.

**ADHD fit:** quick capture reduces working-memory demands. **Friction:** filters, projects and overdue queues can become another system to maintain.

**Price:** free: five personal projects, three filters, seven-day history; Pro $7/month or $5/month annually. [Plans/features](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/todoist-plans-pricing-and-billing-faq-Vq2z0HWL6).

**Access/data:** web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android. Cloud account; [offline after login](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-todoist-while-offline-4rbaZw); [CSV project export excludes completed tasks](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/208821185-Project-templates).

## TickTick

An all-in-one task, calendar and habit manager.

- Lists/Kanban; subtasks; recurrence; task templates.
- Pomodoro/stopwatch; habits; reminders; paid calendar views.

**ADHD fit:** capture and execution tools share one app. **Friction:** numerous views and settings invite tinkering; full calendar planning is paid. [Task features](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782408586526720), [focus timer](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782010496745472).

**Price:** free: nine lists, 99 tasks/list; Premium $49.99/year, approximately $4.17/month; current web page offers annual billing. [Pricing](https://ticktick.com/upgrade).

**Access/data:** web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android. Cloud sync; offline scope unverified; [web backup/export](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055781405648748544).

## Sunsama

A guided daily planner focused on realistic workloads.

- Daily planning/shutdown; weekly review; calendar timeboxing.
- Integrations; subtasks; focus/Pomodoro; automatic rollover.

**ADHD fit:** capacity limits and guided decisions reduce overcommitment. **Friction:** planning rituals require attention; subscriptions exclude casual users. [Features/settings](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/settings/user-settings/).

**Price:** no permanent free tier; 14-day trial; $22/month or $17/month annually. The pricing page’s older FAQ still quotes $25/$20. [Pricing](https://www.sunsama.com/pricing).

**Access/data:** web, desktop, iOS, Android. Cloud-based; [desktop offline remains requested](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/improvements/p/offline-mode-for-desktop-app); CSV/JSON export in settings.

## Motion

An AI scheduler that continuously reprioritizes work.

- Automatic time-blocking/rescheduling; priorities; deadlines; projects.
- Calendar integration; meeting scheduling; AI documents.

**ADHD fit:** reduces repeated “what next?” decisions. **Friction:** estimates and deadlines require setup; moving schedules reduce predictability; overdue warnings can create pressure. [Scheduling](https://www.usemotion.com/features/ai-task-manager), [overdue handling](https://www.usemotion.com/help/time-management/ai-agenda/ai-agenda-how-to-guide).

**Price:** no permanent free tier; trial; Pro AI $29/month or $19/month annually, with AI-credit limits. [Pricing](https://www.usemotion.com/pricing).

**Access/data:** web, Windows/macOS, iOS/Android; cloud account. Offline unverified; [copy tasks as table/list](https://www.usemotion.com/blog/search), rather than a confirmed complete backup.

## Akiflow

A fast task inbox and calendar workspace for busy professionals.

- Integrated capture; command bar; subtasks; recurring time slots.
- Time-blocking; planning rituals; AI assistance; focus timer.

**ADHD fit:** consolidates scattered inputs and supports quick capture. **Friction:** integration setup, keyboard learning and subscription cost.

**Price:** no free tier; seven-day trial requiring a card; $34/month or $19/month annually. [Plans/features](https://akiflow.com/pricing), [timer](https://akiflow.com/features/focus-timer).

**Access/data:** web, Windows/macOS, iOS/Android. Cloud account with [desktop offline support and configurable alerts](https://akiflow.featurebase.app/en/help/articles/0883150-desktop-app); [task export remains planned](https://akiflow.featurebase.app/p/export-tasks).

## Amie

A polished calendar/task planner increasingly centred on AI meeting notes.

- Drag-and-drop tasks; recurrence; integrations; AI scheduling.
- Pomodoro; meeting recording, summaries and action items.

**ADHD fit:** combines appointments and actions. **Friction:** meeting/AI features add complexity beyond personal planning. [Task features](https://amie.so/documentation/features/tasks).

**Price:** current page advertises seven-day trial, Pro $25/month or $20/month annually; no free tier advertised. Help documentation contradicts this, listing free calendars/lists and 25 one-time meeting-note credits. [Pricing](https://amie.so/pricing), [conflicting billing guide](https://amie.so/documentation/account/billing).

**Access/data:** web, macOS, Windows, iPhone; cloud account. Offline unverified; [account data export](https://amie.so/documentation/account/settings).

## Notion

A configurable workspace for documents, databases and planning.

- Boards/timelines; subtasks; templates; reminders.
- Recurring database templates; linked notes; Notion Calendar.

**ADHD fit:** can unite tasks and supporting context. **Friction:** substantial setup, endless customization and sprawling dashboards.

**Price:** free individual pages/blocks; 5 MB uploads, seven-day history; Plus $12/month or $10/month annually. [Pricing/features](https://www.notion.com/pricing).

**Access/data:** web, Windows/macOS, iOS/Android. Cloud account; [downloaded pages work offline in native apps](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/use-pages-offline), with database limits; [HTML, Markdown/CSV and PDF exports](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/export-your-content).

## Tiimo

A visual planner designed around neurodivergent executive-function needs.

- Colour/icon timelines; routines; widgets; visual focus timers.
- AI breakdown, prioritization and estimates; timed subtasks.

**ADHD fit:** makes time and next steps visible. **Friction:** AI dependence, platform differences and paid web access. [Features](https://www.tiimoapp.com/), [subtask timers, September 2026](https://www.tiimoapp.com/resource-hub/timers-on-subtasks).

**Price:** free basic mobile planner, limited AI chats, one profile; US subscriptions approximately $7–14/month or $36–54/year; offers vary. [Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328), [September 2026 period breakdown](https://apppricer.com/app/1480220328).

**Access/data:** iOS/iPadOS/watchOS, Android, web. Cloud account; dependable offline use and self-service export unverified.

## Llama Life

A task-by-task execution companion built around visible time.

- Individual countdowns; projected finish times; gentle chimes.
- Reusable routines; soundscapes; random-task selection; AI breakdown.

**ADHD fit:** lowers starting friction and narrows attention. **Friction:** countdowns may feel pressuring; limited calendar/project planning. [Features](https://llamalife.co/), [breakdown](https://llamalife.co/whatsnew).

**Price:** seven-day trial, no permanent free tier advertised; $6/month or standard $39/year, approximately $3.25/month; expired promotions excluded. [Pricing](https://llamalife.co/pricing).

**Access/data:** browser desktop, iOS/Android. Account/cloud storage; offline guarantees and complete export unverified.

## Structured

A simple visual timeline for organizing each day.

- Timeline; inbox; subtasks; widgets; focus/Pomodoro.
- Paid recurrence, calendar import, AI planning and Replan.

**ADHD fit:** visible gaps and sequencing support time awareness. **Friction:** recurrence is paid; feature parity varies; calendar import is one-way. [Features](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner-todo/id1499198946).

**Price:** free basic planner; Pro $6.99/month, $29.99/year or $99.99 lifetime. [June 2026 pricing](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/324674).

**Access/data:** Apple devices, Android, web. [Local device storage with optional sync](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/1747138); web requires internet/account. [Apple JSON export; no printable PDF export](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/333506).

## Routinery

A guided routine runner that prompts each next action.

- Timed steps; spoken cues; pause/skip/extend.
- Routine templates; schedules; history; timeline.

**ADHD fit:** externalizes transitions and reduces repeated decisions. **Friction:** timers and streak tracking may create pressure; free routines show ads.

**Price:** two free routines; US store lists $5–5.49/month and $36–39.49/year, approximately $3–3.29/month. [Features/pricing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/routine-planner-habit-tracker/id1450486923).

**Access/data:** iOS/iPadOS/watchOS, compatible Apple-silicon Macs, Android; no web planner. Vendor says core routines work offline; server-backed data; no export button, but MCP retrieval available.

## Lunatask

An encrypted personal organizer combining tasks and wellbeing.

- Now/Later prioritization; work-in-progress limits; subtasks.
- Time-blocking; timers; habits; notes; mood/journal.

**ADHD fit:** limits visible commitments without forcing artificial deadlines. **Friction:** multiple workflows and wellbeing tools increase learning demands. [Task model](https://lunatask.app/docs/features/tasks/concepts).

**Price:** free: two areas, seven daily habits, 30 relationships; Premium $8/month or $6/month annually. [Pricing](https://lunatask.app/pricing).

**Access/data:** Windows/macOS/Linux, iOS/Android; no web app. [Encrypted cloud sync with temporary offline use](https://lunatask.app/docs/getting-started/privacy); [desktop export is incomplete](https://lunatask.app/docs/features/import-export).

## Inflow

An ADHD skills and support program adjacent to planners.

- Short CBT-based lessons; challenges; journaling; prioritization.
- Community; expert events; coaching offerings.

**ADHD fit:** explains difficulties and teaches coping strategies. **Friction:** lessons become another commitment; high price; limited scheduling utility. [Program](https://www.getinflow.io/how-it-works).

**Price:** no ongoing free tier; seven-day trial; $47.99/month or $199/year, approximately $16.58/month; affordability support available. [FAQ/pricing](https://www.getinflow.io/faqs).

**Access/data:** iOS/Android, account-based service. Offline coverage and practical journal/task export unverified.

## Goblin Tools

Small AI utilities for overwhelming everyday tasks.

- Magic ToDo breakdown; effort estimates; brain-dump conversion.
- Tone rewriting/interpretation; recipes; adjustable breakdown detail.

**ADHD fit:** turns vague intentions into concrete steps with minimal setup. **Friction:** generated steps require checking; no full calendar or routine runner. [Product](https://goblin.tools/), [limitations](https://goblin.tools/About).

**Price:** core website free without an account; paid native apps; optional Pro $3/month for sync/history/templates. [Pricing](https://goblin.tools/pro/join).

**Access/data:** web, iOS/Android. Browser-local persistence without Pro; AI processing requires internet and sends input to providers. [Privacy](https://goblin.tools/Privacy). Lists support export; Pro adds server storage.

## Patterns worth copying

- Keep Today small: top three commitments, with backlog hidden.
- Show durations, remaining time and realistic capacity.
- Offer editable life-task checklists before requiring AI.
- Support capture first, scheduling later.
- Make rollover explicit, forgiving and reversible.
- Provide optional timers, quiet cues and easy extensions.
- Preserve printable trackers and portable backups.
- Keep basic planning free and usable without registration.

## Patterns to avoid

- Punitive streaks, overdue walls and compulsory scoring.
- Dense dashboards or elaborate setup before first value.
- Filling every available calendar gap automatically.
- Treating missed routines as accumulated debt.
- Annual-renewal surprises after short trials.
- Paywalling essential recurrence or task breakdown.
- Calling cloud caches “local-first.”
- Presenting AI estimates as reliable commitments.
